# Privacy Policy — Dark Mode

_Date: 26.10.2026_

## What data is processed

- **The pages you open.** To recolor a site, the extension works on every page right in the browser: it reads the page's styles and element colors and adds its own styles with dark colors. The content of pages, addresses and browsing history are not recorded or sent anywhere. The extension remembers only what you yourself specified for a site (see below).
- **Your settings.** Whether the extension is on, the default mode, brightness, contrast, sepia, the schedule and the list of sites with your rules (for example, "example.com — do not darken"). A site's address is added to this list only when you yourself turn the theme off or configure it for that site.

## Where the data is stored

Only locally, in your browser's `chrome.storage.local`. The data is not synchronised and is not transmitted to the author or to third parties.

## What the extension does not do

- It sends no network requests at all: no servers, analytics, advertising, telemetry or accounts.
- It does not collect browsing history, page content, forms or passwords.
- It does not load or execute remote code.

## The author's site pages

After installation the extension opens a tab with a short guide on the author's site once, and after removal the browser opens a "why did you uninstall?" page. These are ordinary static pages: the extension adds no data or identifiers to their addresses and transmits nothing to them.

## Export and deletion

- The list of sites can be saved to a .json file and loaded back on the settings page. The file is created right in the browser and saved wherever you choose.
- You can delete all rules with the "Clear list" button in the settings; to delete all data, just remove the extension.
