# Privacy Policy — GOST Cite

_Date: 22.10.2026_

## What data is processed

- **Metadata of the open page.** The extension reads it only after your action: a click on the icon, a context-menu item or a hotkey. These are `<meta>` tags, JSON-LD, the page title, the language and the tab's address. The data is used in the popup to compose a reference.
- **Your bibliographies and settings.** These are the sources you added, list names, the chosen standard and the date format.

## Where the data is stored

The data is stored only locally, in your browser's `chrome.storage.local`. It is not synchronised and is not transmitted to us or to third parties.

## What the extension does not do

- It sends no network requests at all: no servers, analytics, advertising, telemetry or accounts.
- It does not read pages in the background and has no access to all sites. Only the `activeTab` permission is used.
- It does not load or execute remote code.

## The author's site pages

After installation the extension opens a tab with a short guide on the author's site once, and after removal the browser opens a "why did you uninstall?" page. These are ordinary static pages: the extension adds no data or identifiers to their addresses and transmits nothing to them.

## Export and deletion

- .docx, .txt and .json files are created right in the browser and saved wherever you choose.
- You can delete all data on the settings page ("Delete all lists") or simply by removing the extension.
